Why Is Cybersecurity Important?

Cybersecurity protects computers, networks, applications, and personal information from unauthorized access and online threats.

Individuals and businesses should take steps to protect their information and maintain secure online environments.

Five Ways to Stay Safe Online

  1. Use strong and unique passwords.
  2. Enable multi-factor authentication.
  3. Avoid clicking suspicious email links.
  4. Keep software and devices updated.
  5. Never share sensitive personal information with strangers.
